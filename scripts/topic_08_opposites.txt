TOPIC 8: Opposites (Big/Small, Fast/Slow, Up/Down, Hot/Cold)
==============================================================

Rhyme Script:
-------------
Big elephant, small little mouse
Big and small, in this house!
Fast cheetah, slow snail crawls
Fast and slow, up the walls!

Up goes the balloon in the sky
Down comes the rain, falling by
Hot soup warming in the bowl
Cold ice cream, sweet and cold!

Happy face with a great big smile
Sad face crying for a while
Loud drum beating boom boom boom
Quiet whisper in the room

Open the door, come on in
Close the door, let's begin
Daytime bright with sunny light
Nighttime dark with stars so bright!

Opposites everywhere, look and see
How many pairs can you find with me?
Big/Small, Fast/Slow, Up/Down
Hot/Cold, Happy/Sad, Loud/Quiet
Open/Close, Day/Night — opposites are right!
